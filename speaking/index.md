As Unity's Technical Evangelist for Australia and New Zealand I was part of the keynote
at a 500-person conference three years running, ran roadshows on both sides of the
Tasman, and delivered workshops and training to hundreds of developers, including partner
training at the Australian HoloLens 2 launch with Microsoft. I've taught rendering and
game programming to everyone from primary schoolers to conference halls, and teaching is
the part I've never wanted to stop doing.
