---
title: "CultCap: capturing objects with a phone"
description: "A series on building a capture app and Gaussian splat trainer that turns a walk around an object into 3D, for small museums, galleries and anyone with something worth keeping."
---

I'm building a way to turn a walk around an object into a 3D model, using just a phone. A sculpture in a regional gallery, an artefact in a historical society's back room, the thing on the shelf that only has one flat photograph.

These posts follow along as I build it. Some explain how a piece of it works, some are about taking it out into the world, and plenty are about what went wrong.

If you're new, start with [Why I'm doing this](/posts/cultcap-why-im-doing-this/), then see it in action at [the Newcastle Art Gallery](/posts/cultcap-newcastle-art-gallery/).
